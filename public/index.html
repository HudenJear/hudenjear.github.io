COLOR REVERSAL FILMS INTRODUCTION

What is color reversal film (slide film)?

Color reversal film produces a positive image directly on the film, usually processed with E-6. It is known for rich color, high contrast, and a distinctive "projectable" look.

Here I collect notes about major stocks (Velvia, Provia, Ektachrome, etc.), including their characteristics, exposure tips, reciprocity behavior, and availability.

This site focuses exclusively on slide film, and selected frames are available for purchase as original film.
